# Tactical Legends User Documentation

## Overview
Tactical Legends is an immersive strategy game that combines classic tactics with innovative gameplay, set in a fantastical universe featuring unique characters and scenarios.

## Installation
1. Download the Tactical Legends installer from the official website.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation process.
3. Launch the game from your desktop or applications menu.

## Gameplay
Tactical Legends focuses on turn-based strategies where players command their squad in various missions.

## Features
- **Rich Storyline**: Engage in a captivating narrative with unexpected twists.
- **Diverse Characters**: Choose from a range of unique characters, each with their own skills and abilities.
- **Dynamic Environments**: Explore detailed maps that change with each mission.

## Controls
- **Move**: Arrow keys or WASD
- **Select**: Left Mouse Button
- **Perform Action**: Right Mouse Button
- **Open Menu**: ESC key

## Game Modes
- **Single-player Mode**: Experience the campaign solo.
- **Multiplayer Mode**: Team up with friends or play against others online.

## Squad Customization
Players can tailor their squad by selecting characters, enhancing their abilities, and equipping various gear.

## AI System
The game features an advanced AI system that adapts to player strategies, providing a challenging and engaging experience.

## Troubleshooting
If you encounter issues while playing Tactical Legends:
1. Ensure your system meets the minimum requirements.
2. Restart the game and your device if problems persist.
3. Visit our support page for additional help.

## Support Information
For further assistance, contact our support team via email at support@tacticallegends.com or visit our website for FAQs and resources.
